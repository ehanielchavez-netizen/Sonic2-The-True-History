# Credits and sources

**Sonic 2: The True History** is a fan-made campaign mod by **Hanichav02** for
**Sonic 2 Absolute 1.1.1 Easy Setup**.

It includes original editing and integration work, but it also uses zones,
scripts, music, sprites and other material from community projects. Those works
remain the property of their authors. This file records the known sources and
thanks everyone involved.

> This is an unofficial fan project. It is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or
> affiliated with SEGA, Team Forever, or any of the credited authors. Material
> was referenced from public community releases. If any author wants their
> content removed, open an issue and it will be handled in an update.

The original zone and asset authors did not share this material with the project
and did not authorize its inclusion. It comes from public community releases; no
permission or endorsement is claimed.

---

## Base game and engine

**Sonic 2 Absolute — Team Forever**

- Base game, engine, scripts, original zones, menus, and most of the original
  music and sound effects.
- Without Sonic 2 Absolute, this mod would not exist.

## Zones, scripts and assets

### Sonic 2 Absolute: Enchanted Edition

**RealidadeMaxima / AlternateUniverse**

- Wood / Jungle Woods material.
- Ocean Wind material.
- Death Egg and Blue Lake material.
- Jungle Woods, Rocky Winter and Warp Point music.
- Additional assets and references used during development.

### S2A Expanded

- Sand Shower.
- Base for Cyber City / Genocide City.
- `CyberCity_F` music.

### Hydrocity Zone (BETA) and original Hydrocity authors

- Hydrocity level base, used in this mod under the name **Blue Ocean**.
- Hydrocity music and related assets.

### Dust Hill & Genocide City Over SS And CC

- Genocide City and Dust Hill data used as a base and during integration
  testing.
- Original mod by **coolguy98**; S2AE version by **Alice the ironic gaymer
  qween**.

### Sonic Dreamy Skies

- `Super3.ogg` and `Super3K.ogg` music used by the mod.

### Sonic 2 Absolute EXTREME

- Metropolis Acts used during Genocide City 2 testing and development.

### Other community material

- Sonic 3 & Knuckles music and community remixes, credited to their respective
  composers and arrangers.
- Tools, documentation and technical references from the Sonic 2 Absolute
  community.
- Additional assets and music variants kept for the base game, Sound Test, or
  legacy compatibility.

If a specific asset or author is missing from this list, please open an issue
with the details. Corrections can be added in a future release.
